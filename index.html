⚡ The Fundamentals

Nitrogen Rule

Odd M+ = Odd N atoms.
Even M+ = 0 or Even N.

Halogen

Cl: 3:1 ratio at M+2
Br: 1:1 ratio at M+2
Look for twin peaks!

Carbon

#C = (M+1) Intensity / ((M) Intensity * 0.011)

Base Peak: The most intense peak (100% abundance). Represents the most stable fragment, often a 3° carbocation or resonance-stabilized ion.

Other Isotopic Signatures (M+2):

  • Sulfur (34S): Notable 4% peak at M+2.
  • Silicon (30Si): Notable 3% peak at M+2.

🪚 Sigma-Bond Cleavage (Alkanes)

Common in alkanes. Ionization removes an electron from a C-C sigma-bond, weakening it. Fragmentation favors the formation of more stable 3° or 2° carbocations.

🧲 Inductive Cleavage (Halogens)

A heterolytic process common in molecules with highly electronegative atoms (like Halogens). The positive charge "pulls" an electron pair, breaking the bond.
